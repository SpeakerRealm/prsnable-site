The Five Moves

Every build in the course, and every build inside the rooms, runs on the same five moves.

01

Say it plainly

Prompting as brief writing. The quality of what you get is the quality of what you asked for.

02

Build the smallest real version

Not a mockup. The smallest version of the real thing, working.

03

Prove it on screen

Watch it work the way the person receiving it will use it. If a step does not work, it is not done.

04

Publish it

Put it where real people can reach it, on your own domain, under your own name.

05

Learn to run it

A thing you cannot run is a thing you rent. You finish able to operate what you built.
