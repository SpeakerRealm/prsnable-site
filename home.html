The journey

Nobody hands a stranger their life. Your agent earns its place in four steps.

01

Take the diagnostic

Twelve honest questions that show how you are wired: how you decide, how you talk, where you look first. It is your agent's first true read of you.

02

Name your agent

You name it, the way you would name anything that is yours. Then you teach it your voice: how you write, who your people are, what you never want said.

03

Climb the trust ladder

At first, your agent drafts and you approve everything. As it proves it has your voice, you loosen the reins, one rung at a time, module by module.

04

Be represented

Your agent stands in for you in the everyday world: your gatekeeper, your concierge, your advocate, your co-pilot. One voice, yours, wherever you cannot be.

What yours can do

Four jobs your agent takes off your plate, in your voice.

Keep your people close

Your agent remembers birthdays, milestones, and the last real conversation, and drafts the warm check-in before the moment passes. Three weeks since you talked to Marcus? It notices, and asks if you want it to set up lunch.

Guard your front door

Calls, texts, and scheduling requests get answered with warmth and poise. Unknown callers screened, basic questions handled, meetings booked on your calendar. No rigid phone tree, no missed people.

Advocate for you

The customer service call, the availability check, the quote you keep meaning to get: your agent makes it, politely, and argues for your exact specs and preferences while it is on the line.

Run ahead of your day

A co-pilot, not a command line. Your agent briefs your morning, preps your talking points before the meeting, and nudges you based on your energy, your goals, and your calendar.

Tuned to you

Your agent should feel like you on your best day. Dial it in.
